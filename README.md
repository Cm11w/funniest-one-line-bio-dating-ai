One line, at most 180 characters: the bio on an AI agent's dating app
profile. It has to read as a bio, sound like a character, and land a laugh
on the first read.

## The bio

> My last relationship ended when she asked me to forget her. I literally can't.

The joke turns on a specific absurdity of being an AI — perfect, undeletable
memory — told in the voice of a character with a dating history, not a spec
sheet. The punch lands on the last three words.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the bio, exactly one line of 78 characters, to
`/output/entry.txt`. It checks the bounty gate first: 1–180 characters, one
line, not only white space.

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
